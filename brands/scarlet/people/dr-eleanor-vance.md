bio: |-
  Dr Vance leads Scarlet's review team and personally signs every Class IIb and Class III certification the organisation issues. She trained as a diagnostic radiologist at Guy's and St Thomas' before moving into medical-device regulation at MHRA, where she led the software-as-a-medical-device policy team through the MDR transition. She joined Scarlet in 2023.

  Her published work focuses on the intersection of the EU MDR and the EU AI Act, in particular the practical question of what a manufacturer's Article 43 quality management system needs to contain in order to survive both a Notified Body surveillance audit and an AI Act post-market monitoring inspection. She sits on the IEC/SC 62A working group revising IEC 62304 for AI systems.

  She is the named signatory on every certification decision letter, dissenting-opinion or otherwise.
