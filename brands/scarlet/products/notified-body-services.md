# What this service is

Scarlet is the specialist Notified Body for AI and software medical devices. This product is the conformity assessment: you submit a technical file, we review it, and — if the file meets the standard — we issue the certificate of conformity that you rely on to affix the CE mark (EU) or UKCA mark (UK).

Every certification is signed by the reviewer who assessed the file. Every certification page carries the reviewer's name, the certification ID, and the next scheduled surveillance audit.

## What is included

- Review of the technical documentation against `EU MDR Annex II` (or the UK MDR equivalent).
- Assessment of the quality management system against `ISO 13485`.
- Assessment of the software lifecycle against `IEC 62304`.
- Assessment of the risk management file against `ISO 14971`.
- Review of the clinical evaluation report against `EU MDR Annex XIV`.
- Where applicable, coordination with EU AI Act obligations under `Art. 43 §3`.
- Issue of the certificate of conformity and the public certification page on scarlet.cc.
- Scheduled surveillance audits for the certificate's validity period (typically 3 years, subject to annual surveillance).

## What is not included

- The CE mark or the UKCA mark themselves. You affix the mark; we issue the certificate of conformity that lets you do so.
- The clinical evaluation report. We review it; you author it and remain the accountable party for its content.
- Post-market surveillance execution. We audit your surveillance plan and the resulting data; you run the surveillance itself.
- FDA clearance. Scarlet is not an FDA-recognised third party. See `knowledge/regulatory-landscape` for the boundary — we can advise on the harmonised standards, but the US submission is yours.
