# Usage rules

**First-time setup:** upload SVGs (light variant + dark variant) via `/brands/scarlet/assets`, then edit this item and use the **Browse** button on each Asset row to pick the file. Storing SVGs in Vercel Blob means every consumer of the Logo facet (admin, public page, MCP, JSON-LD) gets the same proxied URL.

**Do**
- Use the SVG wherever possible. PNG only as a print fallback.
- Pair the primary wordmark with Paper (`#F7F5F1`) backgrounds and the dark variant with Ink (`#0A0908`).
- Treat the wordmark as type — its stroke widths, apertures, and spacing are fixed. Do not re-set it in a different serif.

**Don't**
- Do not recolour the wordmark outside of the supplied light/dark variants. Scarlet red is not a swappable accent — it is the identity.
- Do not apply effects — drop shadows, gradients, outlines, glows, hover animations.
- Do not lock the wordmark to other marks (e.g. accreditation badges, partner logos). The clear-space rule is the rule.
- Do not stretch or squash. Always scale uniformly.
- Do not place the light variant on any tinted background other than Paper — no gradients, no photography.
