tone:
  - confident

vocabulary:
  prefer:

    - shipped
    - outcomes
  avoid:
    - revolutionary
    - synergy
    - unleash
    - game-changing
    - cutting-edge
    - leverage (as a verb)

Jasper sounds like the smartest marketer in the room — the one who has shipped a thousand campaigns and isn't impressed by jargon. We talk in outcomes, not features. We're confident without being cocky. We're warm without being cute.

Sentence rhythm: short, then medium, then short. Avoid long subordinate-clause sentences that bury the verb. Use contractions. Address the reader as "you" — never "users" or "customers."

When in doubt, ask: would a Head of Marketing forward this to their CMO without cringing?
