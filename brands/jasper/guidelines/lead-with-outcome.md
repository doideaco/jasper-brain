---
id: lead-with-outcome
name: Lead with the outcome, not the feature
description: Open every piece of marketing copy with the result the reader gets, not the mechanism that delivers it.
tags: [copywriting, structure]
scope: All outbound marketing copy — emails, landing pages, ads, social. Not required for in-product UX copy or technical documentation.
examples:
  do:
    - "Ship a month of on-brand content in an afternoon."
    - "Stop rewriting the same brief for every channel."
  dont:
    - "Jasper uses a proprietary multi-model orchestration layer to..."
    - "Our new Brand Voice 2.0 module now supports..."
---

The reader doesn't care how it works. They care what changes for them after they buy it.

Every headline, hero subhead, and email opener should answer "what becomes true for me?" — not "what does the product do?" If the first sentence could appear unchanged in a competitor's copy, rewrite it.

Features belong further down the page, after the reader has decided the outcome is worth their attention.
