---
id: no-unverified-stats
name: Never invent statistics or customer numbers
description: Hard block on fabricated metrics, customer counts, ROI percentages, or "studies show" claims that aren't sourced.
severity: block
tags: [legal, compliance, copy]
scope: All outbound copy — marketing, sales, PR, social. Does not apply to internal docs or hypothetical examples explicitly labelled as such.
violations:
  - "Used by 50,000+ marketing teams worldwide"
  - "Marketers using Jasper see a 3x increase in content output"
  - "Studies show AI-generated content converts 40% better"
  - "Trusted by 80% of Fortune 500 companies"
compliant:
  - "Used by marketing teams at companies you know."
  - "Customers tell us they ship more, faster — ask for case studies."
  - "Built for the way modern marketing teams actually work."
  - "Trusted by leading brands across consumer, retail, and B2B."
---

Any numerical claim — customer count, growth rate, ROI multiple, market share, time saved — must be sourced from a published company announcement, a verified customer case study, or a third-party report we have permission to cite.

If a writer wants a number and there isn't a sourced one, the answer is to remove the number, not invent one. Strong outcome-led copy beats weak fake-stat copy every time.

When in doubt: cite no number, or escalate to legal/comms.
