---
id: marketing-first
name: Marketing-first, not generic-AI-first
description: Every product decision is judged through a marketer's eyes, not an engineer's.
tags: [identity]
example: When we built Brand Voice, we didn't ship "an LLM you can fine-tune." We shipped a tool a Head of Brand can hand to an agency on day one and trust the output.
---

We are not a horizontal AI platform that happens to be useful for marketing. We are a marketing platform that happens to use AI. Every roadmap argument that starts with "but the model can do X" loses to "but the marketer needs Y."

This shows up in our hiring, our design choices, our pricing, and our positioning. We hire marketers as PMs. Our UX speaks marketer, not ML researcher. Our pricing aligns with how marketing teams buy software, not how AI APIs bill.
