---
id: plain-over-clever
name: Plain over clever
description: We choose the clearer word, the shorter sentence, the obvious metaphor — every time.
tags: [voice, identity]
example: We named our context layer "Signal" not "Cognitive Workspace" or "Knowledge Graph Platform." Because Signal is what it is.
---

Cleverness flatters the writer. Plainness serves the reader. In every piece of marketing copy, product naming, and customer conversation, we lean toward what is immediately understandable — even when something more elaborate would feel more sophisticated.

The test: read the line aloud. If you'd be slightly embarrassed to say it to a friend in a coffee shop, rewrite it.
